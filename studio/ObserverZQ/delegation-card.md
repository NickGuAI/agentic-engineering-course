# Delegation Card

## Task
What specific artifact or result are you delegating to the AI?

## Context
What relevant inputs, files, tools, target audience, or learning preferences should the AI consider?

Categorize articles from different websites based on their editorial labels. Combine labels that have similar meanings in AI industry. The report shows the categories alphabetically.
Run the task every 7:00 AM EST every Monday. Test runs should be separated. The AI news should be be sourced and integrated from mainly these three websites. No minimum per source. The summary word count should be no more than 300 words, excluding titles, labels, links, dates, etc. 
1.https://www.anthropic.com/news
2.https://www.anthropic.com/engineering
3.https://openai.com/news/

## Success criteria
What is the observable check or metric that defines a successful run?
Each development should contain a title, a summary, keyword/label attached below. All the headlines and summaries have specific link address quoting the article on one of the three websites.

## Restrictions
Are there any prohibited actions, resource limits, or human approval boundaries to enforce?
All summary must be based on actual content from the articles. No fabricated news or content.
