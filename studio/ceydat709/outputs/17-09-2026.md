### How we contain Claude across products
Published May 25, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude
- Examines how to cap the "blast radius" of increasingly capable agents across claude.ai, Claude Code, and Cowork.
- Telemetry showed users approve about 93% of permission prompts, with attention dropping over time — human-in-the-loop supervision alone is fallible.

- Argues containment (bounding an agent's access/environment) is a more robust defense than supervision alone.

### Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands
Published Apr 08, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents
- Introduces Managed Agents, a hosted Claude Platform service for long-horizon agent work, designed so its interfaces stay stable as harnesses evolve.
- Motivated by harness assumptions going stale as models improve — e.g. a "context anxiety" workaround built for Sonnet 4.5 was unnecessary on Opus 4.5.
- Draws an analogy to OS abstractions (like files and processes) that outlive the hardware implementations beneath them.
- Virtualizes an agent into three parts: a session (append-only log), a harness (the call/tool-routing loop), and a sandbox (execution environment).
