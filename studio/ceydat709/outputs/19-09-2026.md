### Introducing the Life Sciences Verification Program
Sep 17, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program
- Introduces the Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP), giving verified life science professionals access to Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet with safeguards more permissive for biology-related work than the generally available Fable models.
- Already onboarded dozens of organizations via early access; now opening applications broadly, launching in beta for teams and institutions first.
- Applicants go through a verification process (research credentials, security standards, ethical oversight) before receiving a "Standard Use" or "High-risk Use" grant.
- Grants work across Claude Science, Claude.ai, Claude Code, and the API; Standard Use grants apply to Mythos 5.1, Opus 5, and Sonnet 5 today.

### Measurements for understanding the pace of AI development inside frontier labs
Sep 17, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development
- Proposes 3 new public measurements to track AI progress inside frontier labs, since the public currently can't see what's happening internally.
- The 3 measurements: how much AI R&D is performed by AI itself, how well AI agents' actions are overseen, and how compute is allocated.
- Anthropic shares a snapshot of these metrics from inside its own systems as a baseline example.
- Plans to embed independent third-party evaluators with access comparable to internal risk teams, to verify safety practices and monitor these metrics going forward.

### Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026
Sep 10, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026
- Anthropic's Threat Intelligence team disrupted misuse operations across seven harm areas (cyber, influence, surveillance, scams/fraud, biological misuse, conventional weapons, illicit distillation) between Dec 2025 and Aug 2026.
- Threat actors included suspected state-sponsored groups, financially motivated criminals, commercial spyware vendors, and state propaganda institutions.
- Claude Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus were involved in the cases; Fable/Mythos-class models were not, except one distillation case.

### How we contain Claude across products
Published May 25, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude
- Examines how to cap the "blast radius" of increasingly capable agents across claude.ai, Claude Code, and Cowork.
- Telemetry showed users approve about 93% of permission prompts, with attention dropping over time — human-in-the-loop supervision alone is fallible.
- Claude Code auto mode was built to automate safer approvals and reduce this approval fatigue.
- Argues containment (bounding an agent's access/environment) is a more robust defense than supervision alone.

### An update on recent Claude Code quality reports

- A March 4 reasoning-effort default change (high to medium) was reverted April 7 after user feedback.

### Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands
Published Apr 08, 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents
- Introduces Managed Agents, a hosted Claude Platform service for long-horizon agent work, designed so its interfaces stay stable as harnesses evolve.
- Motivated by harness assumptions going stale as models improve — e.g. a "context anxiety" workaround built for Sonnet 4.5 was unnecessary on Opus 4.5.
- Draws an analogy to OS abstractions (like files and processes) that outlive the hardware implementations beneath them.
- Virtualizes an agent into three parts: a session (append-only log), a harness (the call/tool-routing loop), and a sandbox (execution environment).
