# Delegation Card

## Task

Create a markdown file, reporting on the three newest posts from https://www.anthropic.com/news , https://www.anthropic.com/engineering , https://openai.com/news/ . In your report, also include a list of top five most referenced buzzwords/terminology (sorted by occurence) that are shared between the posts. 

## Context

The audience for this is Columbia University masters students, so feel free to be technical in your reporting.

## Success criteria

All of the following are needed for a successful run:
- All post reports should be within one file
- Reports should be digestible, so no more than 4 sentences per post
- Dates, post titles, and authors should be included in the report per-post
- Each post report should be grounded in the actual post

## Restrictions

- Do not access websites outside of the ones listed in the task section. 

## Clarifications after teammate review

- Select the three newest posts from each listed section, for nine posts total.
- When a post has no named author, report `No author listed` rather than inferring one.
- A candidate term must occur in at least two selected posts. Rank terms first by the number of selected posts containing the term, then by total case-insensitive occurrences. Exclude ordinary stop words, publisher names, and navigation or footer text.
