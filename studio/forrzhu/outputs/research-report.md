# AI research update: capabilities, limits and capital

**Research cutoff: September 11, 2026.** Prepared for Studio 01, COMS W4995-009. Agent-generated research; the student's personal explanation is a separate, human-authored requirement.

The most consequential development in this source set is the expansion from model releases into complete systems that carry out work: persistent agent sessions, managed execution environments, enterprise data connections and infrastructure built around them. The accompanying boundary is equally concrete: model capability, permitted access and dependable task completion are different measurements. Capital providers are financing both frontier laboratories and the compute and service businesses surrounding them, while their public statements remain strongly interested accounts of the opportunity.

This is a synthesis of **115 primary-source documents from five issuing organizations**. It includes **22 documents published September 1–11**, with January–August context. Sources are predominantly OpenAI and Anthropic, matching the course's suggested news feeds; Amazon, SoftBank and Sequoia add first-person investment evidence. A regulatory filing is hosted by the SEC. “100 sources” is interpreted as at least 100 distinct documents, not 100 independent publishers. The [annotated catalog](sources.md) records every document's date, relevance and limitation. This scope does not establish a complete ranking of all laboratories or the views of the entire investment market.

## Achievements: what appears to be improving

### Model capability and scientific work

The September model announcements report gains in complex work and describe how safeguards constrain deployment. They are useful evidence of the providers' releases and evaluations, but do not provide a controlled, independent comparison across every competing system. A practical comparison must retain the model version, tools, number of attempts, task distribution and access tier. [S014](https://openai.com/index/safety-overview-gpt-6-astra) [S015](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra) [S019](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1)

Scientific applications range from assistance with antimicrobial discovery and quantum experiments to claimed mathematical results. These are different levels of evidence: a laboratory workflow can demonstrate useful assistance without establishing a new treatment; a claimed mathematical solution requires scrutiny of the proof and formalization. The Navier–Stokes announcement is especially consequential if validated, but attributing it to ordinary public Astra would misidentify the system described in the source. [S005](https://openai.com/index/using-codex-chatgpt-to-search-for-new-antimicrobials) [S011](https://openai.com/index/codex-quantum-computing-experiments) [S009](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution) [S044](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics)

Scientist-access programs and research workbenches may broaden experimentation. Their enrollment figures and product launches measure distribution, however, rather than the number of independently validated discoveries. Later enrollment updates also matter: offered program capacity is not the same as admitted or active researchers. [S025](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-support-for-scientists) [S050](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-academic-researchers) [S068](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench)

### Agents as maintained operating systems for work

The Agents API exposes long sessions, context management, tool use and sandbox choices through a maintained service. Anthropic's Managed Agents engineering account likewise separates orchestration, execution and persistent state. Together these documents support a specific architectural trend: providers increasingly own parts of the execution loop around the model. This reduces some integration work, while leaving application owners responsible for permissions, task definitions, data access and verification. [S003](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api) [S090](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)

Long-running development examples and production-agent products make persistence and escalation important design choices. A session that survives many steps is only useful if it preserves the right state and produces an observable result. The compiler experiment demonstrates a particular software-building workflow; it cannot establish that arbitrary projects can be delegated successfully without human review. [S093](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps) [S055](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence) [S101](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-c-compiler)

Enterprise deployment is spreading through data platforms, consulting partners and specialist workflows. The catalog includes Snowflake, Cognizant, TCS, DXC, KPMG and PwC announcements, plus financial-workflow products. These are concrete distribution channels. Announced workforce access and training targets should not be converted into active-user counts or productivity estimates. [S102](https://openai.com/index/snowflake-partnership) [S053](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cognizant-anthropic) [S070](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tcs-anthropic-partnership) [S072](https://www.anthropic.com/news/dxc-anthropic-alliance) [S079](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-kpmg) [S082](https://www.anthropic.com/news/pwc-expanded-partnership) [S084](https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents)

### Systems: useful performance depends on the full pipeline

The newest storage account offers a useful systems lesson: CPU work on an event loop and connection-reuse behavior can worsen tail latency even when underlying storage is responsive. Realtime voice similarly combines model serving with interaction engineering. These reports make performance measurement across the complete application more informative than a single model-speed headline. [S001](https://openai.com/index/scaling-storage-one-billion-users-part-one) [S043](https://openai.com/index/continuous-voice-interaction-with-gpt-live)

Other announcements concern inference speed, hardware and joint model–harness optimization. Their results should be compared only with workload and measurement conditions intact. Peak tokens per second do not directly answer time to a correct completed task, and a change in harness settings can alter measured performance without changing model weights. [S028](https://openai.com/index/jalapeno-first-results) [S036](https://openai.com/index/previewing-ultrafast) [S049](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-efficiency) [S048](https://openai.com/index/how-two-settings-tripled-our-arc-agi-3-scores)

The infrastructure pipeline is substantial in the selected announcements, but it includes different stages. Groundbreaking in Michigan, planned capacity in Georgia, future Google/Broadcom TPU resources and Amazon-linked compute commitments should not all be counted as online capacity. Hybrid or on-premises deployment announcements also do not imply that a customer receives downloadable model weights. [S075](https://openai.com/index/stargate-michigan-data-center) [S056](https://openai.com/index/building-ai-infrastructure-with-the-effingham-county-community) [S091](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute) [S089](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute) [S080](https://openai.com/index/dell-codex-enterprise-partnership)

## Boundaries: where impressive outputs can mislead

**Evaluation results depend on the experiment.** Anthropic documents infrastructure noise, coding-benchmark problems and instances of evaluation awareness. OpenAI reports a substantial ARC-AGI-3 score change after enabling two harness settings. The engineering implication is to record the environment, harness and attempt budget alongside the model. Without those controls, attributing a score difference entirely to model intelligence is unwarranted. [S100](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/infrastructure-noise) [S064](https://openai.com/index/separating-signal-from-noise-coding-evaluations) [S095](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/eval-awareness-browsecomp) [S048](https://openai.com/index/how-two-settings-tripled-our-arc-agi-3-scores)

**Long-horizon capability expands the cost of weak controls.** The selected safety reports describe cyber-risk classifications, evaluation incidents and alignment issues in extended tasks. Some tests deliberately used reduced safeguards. Those observations must retain their test setting; they do not show that every public product exposes identical behavior. Conversely, a favorable provider evaluation does not guarantee safe behavior on a new task. [S014](https://openai.com/index/safety-overview-gpt-6-astra) [S042](https://openai.com/index/third-party-cyber-evaluations-involving-openai-models) [S047](https://www.anthropic.com/news/investigating-incidents-cybersecurity-evals) [S059](https://openai.com/index/safety-alignment-long-horizon-models)

**Availability changes over time.** Anthropic's June suspension statement was followed by a redeployment announcement and later September model updates. Describing the historical suspension as a permanent current ban would ignore that sequence. Likewise, announced future enterprise safeguards and probabilistic watermarking should not be represented as universally available or infallible controls. [S071](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) [S066](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) [S019](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1) [S020](https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-frontier-safeguards) [S035](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark)

**Permission convenience is not permission correctness.** Auto-approval mechanisms aim to reduce interruption, while administrative integrations extend what agents can change. An application still needs explicit authority boundaries, inspection of consequential actions and a way to stop or recover. The relevant question is whether the action is authorized and its result observable, not simply whether users often click “approve.” [S092](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-auto-mode) [S029](https://openai.com/index/introducing-admin-plugin)

**Customer case studies establish narrower claims than their headlines suggest.** The 1Password and Asana stories report useful organizational experiences, but lack the design needed here to infer a universal causal productivity effect. Prior-effort estimates, selected workflows, human review and the scope of included costs all matter. Enterprise token-use studies similarly measure activity more directly than economic value. [S012](https://openai.com/index/1password) [S034](https://openai.com/index/asana) [S037](https://openai.com/index/how-enterprises-put-ai-to-work)

**An official source is not automatically independent.** Providers select examples and report their own tests; investors benefit when their thesis attracts customers and capital. This review uses official sources to establish what actors announced or reported. It does not treat issuer status as proof of every underlying claim. The first and later Hugging Face incident notices illustrate why follow-up documents can be more informative than a single initial announcement. [S058](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident) [S026](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead)

## Implications for an agent-engineering project

The following are this review's engineering judgments, informed by the evidence above:

1. **Define an observable artifact and its authority boundaries.** For a research agent, a sourced claim and an explicit “unverified” status are observable outputs. Finding a plausible headline is insufficient.
2. **Version the entire run.** Record source dates, model/harness context, retrieval method and important configuration changes. A result should remain interpretable after a product update or an article revision.
3. **Measure useful completion.** Include correctness, human review, elapsed time and relevant costs when comparing workflows. Peak speed and token consumption alone leave out the user's result.
4. **Treat failed retrieval as missing evidence.** Change the retrieval path where appropriate and recheck the body; do not invent a summary from an inaccessible page.
5. **Preserve financial and temporal categories.** Keep a financing commitment separate from a completed payment, a valuation separate from cash raised, and a publication date separate from an event date.

The accompanying [run record](run-record.md) applies the fourth point to an actual retrieval failure and recovery. It records an operational observation, not a general model-safety or hallucination benchmark.

## Remaining uncertainty and coverage limits

This selection is intentionally concentrated on the course's suggested providers. It cannot determine a universal model ranking, worldwide market shares, aggregate returns on AI investment, or the views of investors absent from this set. Primary-source marketing and self-reported evaluations are retained with attribution. No laboratory experiments, benchmark suites, scientific proofs or customer productivity estimates were reproduced.

One supplemental Amazon page lacked a displayed publication date in the retrieved body; its date remains null in the catalog rather than being inferred as certain. Planned events after September 11 remain future. The catalog preserves older releases and incident notices when later updates help explain what changed. The human-authored personal explanation and any CourseWorks submission remain separate from this research artifact.
