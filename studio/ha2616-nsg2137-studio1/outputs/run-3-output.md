## OpenAI

- **[Introducing GPT‑Rosalind for life sciences research](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-rosalind/)** — Published **April 16, 2026**; updated **September 11, 2026**; **carried forward**. The original research evidence includes outperforming GPT‑5.4 on six of eleven LABBench2 tasks and an evaluation using unpublished RNA sequences; those results are not newly dated September findings.
