## Introducing Astra for Law

2026-09-17 | openai-news | [Source](https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/)

OpenAI announced a legal-work configuration of GPT-6 Astra combining the model with legal search, specialized instructions, and controls for confidential work. Initial access is for selected firms through ChatGPT and Codex, with API access planned; an API lets software interact with the model programmatically.

## Introducing the Life Sciences Verification Program

2026-09-17 | anthropic-news | [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program)

Anthropic announced a beta program giving verified life-science teams access to models with safeguards adjusted for biology work. Applicants are reviewed, and access is tied to specified uses with ongoing monitoring.

## Measurements for understanding the pace of AI development inside frontier labs

2026-09-17 | anthropic-news | [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development)

Anthropic proposes measuring how much AI research is automated, how agents are monitored, and how computing resources are allocated. The company presents internal measurements and discusses independent verification and limits to comparisons between labs.

## Our framework for reporting model misalignment

2026-09-16 | openai-news | [Source](https://openai.com/index/model-misalignment-reporting-framework/)

OpenAI introduced a process for investigating and publishing reports of model misalignment: behavior that departs from intended goals or constraints. It released six examples and cautioned that individual incidents do not establish how frequently these behaviors occur.

## Reimagining advertising with AI

2026-09-16 | openai-news | [Source](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/)

OpenAI is testing Sponsored Agents, clearly labeled conversations with business-sponsored agents after an ad click. It also announced tools for creating and managing ads, plus integrations with HubSpot and Shopify.
