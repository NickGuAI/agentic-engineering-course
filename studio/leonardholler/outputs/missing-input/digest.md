2026-09-17 | anthropic-news | [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program)

## Measurements for understanding the pace of AI development inside frontier labs

2026-09-17 | anthropic-news | [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development)

Anthropic proposes measuring how much AI research is automated, how agents are monitored, and how computing resources are allocated. The company presents internal measurements and discusses independent verification and limits to comparisons between labs.
