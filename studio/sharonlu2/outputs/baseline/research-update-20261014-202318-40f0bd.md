### 4. Our approach to EU text provenance rules

- **Publication date:** 2026-10-08
- **Source:** OpenAI News
- **Article:** [https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance/](https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance/)
- **Summary:** OpenAI describes textGrain, a statistical watermark in model word choices, and says it will roll it out for eligible ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU while allowing API customers globally to opt in; detector access will initially be limited to approved researchers and expert organizations. In its evaluations, detection fell for shorter or constrained text and after synonym edits, and a watermark does not establish authorship, ownership, accuracy, or user identity.

### 9. Asana cuts model costs 76x in browser tests with GPT‑6.1 Sol

- **Publication date:** 2026-10-08
- **Source:** OpenAI News
- **Article:** [https://openai.com/index/asana-browser-agent/](https://openai.com/index/asana-browser-agent/)
- **Summary:** In a 144-run study, Asana used GPT-6 Astra in Codex to optimize a browser-agent workflow; the resulting GPT-6.1 Sol setup averaged $0.47 and about four minutes per run, reported as 76 times cheaper and five times faster than the original production setup. The changes included caching browsing history, retaining more text, and removing screenshots in batches; all optimized runs completed the task correctly.
