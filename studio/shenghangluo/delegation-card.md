# Delegation Card

## Task

What specific artifact or result are you delegating to the AI?

Create a concise course-assistant digest for this module: a 1-page study guide plus 1–2 visual aids (concept map, comparison table, or flowchart) that summarizes the key ideas, definitions, and connections between course concepts in a format tailored to a visual learner. The artifact should be easy to scan and useful for review before class, assignments, or an exam.

## Context

What relevant inputs, files, tools, target audience, or learning preferences should the AI consider?

- Course materials: lecture notes, readings, slides, and assignment prompts from the current module.
- Learning style: visual-first, concise, structured, and practical.
- Audience: me and my teammate reviewing the material together.
- Preferred format: bullet summaries, diagrams, example-based explanations, and a short “what to remember” section.
- Tooling: the AI can read provided course materials and generate a summary without needing to browse the web.

## Success criteria

What is the observable check or metric that defines a successful run?

- The output is easy to understand in under 10 minutes.
- Includes the main concepts, definitions, and relationships between them.
- Includes at least one visual element that helps explain the material.
- Highlights likely test or discussion points and gives brief examples when helpful.
- Stays grounded in the provided course content and does not add unsupported claims.

## Restrictions

Are there any prohibited actions, resource limits, or human approval boundaries to enforce?

- Do not invent lecture topics, reading details, or assignment requirements.
- Keep the summary grounded in the provided course materials only.
- Do not produce a long-form essay; use a compact, scan-friendly format.
- If the material is missing or unclear, ask for clarification before making assumptions.
