# Delegation Card

## Task
What specific artifact or result are you delegating to the AI?
We are asking the AI to create a research brief pulling news from Anthropic News, Anthropic Engineering, and OpenAI News.

## Context
What relevant inputs, files, tools, target audience, or learning preferences should the AI consider?
Inputs: We are providing the websites from which we want the AI to pull data including: https://www.anthropic.com/news, https://www.anthropic.com/engineering, https://openai.com/news/. 
We are also providing a simple template for how we want the brief to look. 
Target Audience: the target audience will be students looking to keep up-to-date with AI.
Learning preferences: Keep it brief, don't provide too much text. 

## Success criteria
What is the observable check or metric that defines a successful run?
Success metric: If the agent successfully matches our provided template and correctly scrapes recent data from the websites provided. 
Also, does not pull data from other websites. 

## Restrictions
Are there any prohibited actions, resource limits, or human approval boundaries to enforce?
No.
