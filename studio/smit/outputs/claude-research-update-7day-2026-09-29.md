### Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats

- **Source/date:** Anthropic News — 2026-09-23
- **Summary:** Anthropic's life sciences research group used Claude agents to autonomously search over 200,000 reverse transcriptases in DNA sequence data, flag interesting candidates, and identify a previously uncharacterized enzyme system (array-associated reverse transcriptases, "ART") with CRISPR-like repeat patterns, found mainly in bacteriophages. Researchers then validated the finding in the lab.
- **Why it matters:** It is a concrete, lab-validated example of AI agents moving from large-scale data search to a testable biological hypothesis, illustrating a workflow that could generalize to other data-heavy research domains.
- **Link:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system
