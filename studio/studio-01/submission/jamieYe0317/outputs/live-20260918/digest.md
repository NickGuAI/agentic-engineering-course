## 1. Introducing the Life Sciences Verification Program

Published: 2026-09-17 · [Publisher source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program)

Anthropic says its beta Life Sciences Verification Program gives verified teams access to models with more permissive biology safeguards. Access is available through its first-party API console and Claude Enterprise and Team plans; individual plans, third-party platforms, and BAA-enabled organizations are excluded. The program monitors usage against approved use cases and requires 30-day data retention, with retained data excluded from model training.
