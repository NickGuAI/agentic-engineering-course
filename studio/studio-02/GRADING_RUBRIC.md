# Studio 02 Grading Rubric

This grading rubric outlines the 10-point total grading system for evaluating student group submissions of Studio 02.

| Section | Criterion | Points | Grading Standard | What to Watch For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Completion - Part A** | Part A Evidence & Reproducibility | 2 | Full points if all required evidence files are present, their contents are internally consistent with Sections 1 and 2 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md`, and the exact runs are reproducible using the specified command and model (`openai/gpt-5.6-luna` or the alternative ChatGPT Plus/Pro login `openai-codex/gpt-5.6-luna`). | Confirm the presence of `evidence/part_a/results.json`, `results.csv`, and `summary.md`. Ensure results show evaluation across the 256K, 512K, and 768K buckets with `--n` (default 5) items per bucket. Verify that no placeholder text is left and that no API keys or secrets are committed. |
| **Completion - Part B** | Part B Evidence & Reproducibility | 2 | Full points if all required evidence files are present, their contents are internally consistent with Section 3 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md`, and the exact runs are reproducible using the specified command and model. | Confirm the presence of `evidence/part_b/results.json`, `results.csv`, `summary.md`, and `comparison.png`. Ensure results include both Isolate and Targeted summary runs on the same items that Part A scored. Verify that peak single-call context tokens and total tokens are both tracked, demonstrating that peak single-call context is successfully capped (e.g., around ~96,000-token chunks) instead of holding the entire story at once. |
| **Completion - Part C** | Part C Evidence & Reproducibility | 2 | Full points if all required files are present, their contents are internally consistent with Section 4 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md`, and the exact runs are reproducible using the specified command and model. | Confirm the presence of `evidence/part_c/decisions.md`, `fib.py`, `session3-with-memory-answer.md`, `session3-no-memory-answer.md`, `part_c.json` (completed automatic recall check), and `summary.md`. Verify that `decisions.md` contains dated, sequential entries tracking 3 decisions for Session 1 (storage format, command name, date format) and at least 1 decision for Session 2. Confirm that Session 3 answers with and without memory are present. |
| **Explanation - Part A** | Correct Causal Account of Part A Degradation | 1 | Full points if Section 2 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md` provides a correct causal account of why and where the model's accuracy degraded as input size increased. | Look for technical analysis explaining attention dilution/context issues as token sizes increased across the 256K, 512K, and 768K buckets, supported by actual metrics from their runs. Reject vague answers or those that do not cite real numbers from their evidence. |
| **Explanation - Part B** | Correct Attribution of Part B Fix | 1 | Full points if Section 3 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md` correctly attributes the success of the Part B fixes to the right operation. | Confirm the explanation attributes the improvement to the reduction of peak single-call context (capping single-call context to fixed ~96,000-token chunks) under Isolate and/or Targeted summary. Ensure they cite accuracy scores and token counts before and after the fix. |
| **Explanation - Part C** | Memory Assessment & Baseline Comparison | 1 | Full points if Section 4 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md` contains an honest assessment of what memory got right/wrong and a real comparison with the baseline. | Check that the team compares the with-memory run results directly to the memoryless baseline (Session 3 prompt in the fresh baseline folder). They must specify which details were correctly recalled, missed, or hallucinated using the `decisions.md` and `AGENTS.md` context vs. the empty/missing baseline, admitting honest unknowns. |
| **Explanation - Group Contributions** | Individual Contribution Paragraphs | 1 | Full points if Section 6 of `evidence/EXPLANATION.md` contains a specific, individual contribution paragraph from every single student group member. | Ensure every member of the group has a unique, specific paragraph describing their individual role and work in the studio. Do not award this point if any member's paragraph is missing, or if they are generic, identical, or combined. |

## Spot-Check Instructions

During grading, the TAs will select one group member at random to explain a specific run from their submitted evidence. The selected student must be able to explain how the command was executed, what model was used, and interpret the resulting evidence (such as the token counts, Isolate sub-agent reports, or decisions recorded in memory) to verify that the group understands and actively participated in the studio.

## Late or Missing Submissions

If a submission is late, missing, or there are any ambiguous situations regarding student grading policies, the TAs should flag the case and escalate it to the instructor, Nick Gu, for clarification rather than attempting to guess a policy.
