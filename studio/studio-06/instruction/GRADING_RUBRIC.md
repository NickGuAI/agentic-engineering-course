# Studio 06 Grading Rubric

**Total: 10 points.** Each row earns its listed points when the condition is met.

| Line | Points | Condition for credit | Read from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation | 8 | Your deep research agent actually runs at least five research sub-agents searching in parallel. | Implementation and parallel-run evidence |
| Comparison | 2 | Run both modes on all three tasks, score the six reports with RACE, and compare the results. | Six reports, original RACE outputs, and the comparison in `EXPLANATION.md` |

There are no additional checkpoints or explanation points. There is no minimum RACE score or class ranking.

The grader reads your team's private repository at the submitted commit. It does not run your code. Evidence that needs interpretation goes to a TA.
