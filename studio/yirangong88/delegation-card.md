# Delegation Card

## Task
Have a daily AI news newsletter. 
Qualifying updates published in the last natural day from the execution day (EST). 
For example, if I ran the execution on Sept 17, it should give me all the news on Sept 16. 


## Context
Input/source: anthropic news, openai news. 
Preferences: bullet points, ask 2 questions in the end to help me think and actually engage with the news. 
Target audience: someone that want's to be an AI product manager and AI engineer. It needs to touch on the product decisions behind certain moves; while also convering technical aspects of new launches if relevant. 

## Success criteria
All news are accurate and within the previous 24hrs. 
Factual, with link to the original message. 

## Restrictions
Do not add information not relevant to tech. 
Do not invent facts.
