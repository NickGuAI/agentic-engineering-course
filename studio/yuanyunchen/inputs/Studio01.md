# Studio 01: Delegation & the first observable run

**Workshop Duration:** 75 minutes.

Ensure you have completed the setup in [Course Prep](../docs/course-prep.md).

## 1. Frame

Before starting, review the workspace instructions in [README.md](README.md). Create your team or individual folder under `studio/`, copy [delegation-card.md](delegation-card.md) into it, and fill out the card for one of the following choices:

- **Research Update:** Latest AI news from [Anthropic News](https://www.anthropic.com/news), [Anthropic Engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering), or [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/).
- **Course Assistant:** Digest course material tailored to your learning style (visual if useful; does not have to be a chatbot).

**Teammate game (7 minutes):**

- 2 min: Write.
- 2 min: Swap, paraphrase, identify ambiguity.
- 2 min: Revise.
- 1 min: Debrief.

## 2. Run & Inspect

Run local Codex or Claude Code on your bounded, recurring job using your own account (no credentials in GitHub). Inspect the output and the execution trace.

## 3. Change & Explain

Change one condition (missing input, failed check, or permission boundary). Observe, make an evidence-led correction, and compare results (responsible stop allowed).

Preserve all code, outputs, and your delegation card in your chosen folder under `studio/`. Each student must personally write their own separate explanation file, without AI-generated text (`explanation-<username>.md`). Submit your work by opening a pull request to `main` following the workflow in [README.md](README.md). Once the PR is merged, clean up by deleting your working branch. Remember that official submissions and grading deadlines are managed exclusively through CourseWorks.
